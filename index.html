Common Questions

Are these products safe for human consumption?
All products sold by Neo Peptide are strictly for research purposes only and are not intended for human consumption, diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any disease or condition. These compounds are sold exclusively to licensed researchers and research institutions.
How can I obtain a Certificate of Analysis (COA)?
A Certificate of Analysis is available for all products upon request. Simply contact our support team with your order number and the specific product(s) you need the COA for. We'll send the third-party lab results directly to your email within 24 hours.
What shipping methods do you offer?
We offer standard (3-5 business days) and expedited (1-2 business days) shipping within the USA. All orders are shipped in discreet, unmarked packaging with insulated containers to preserve compound stability. Free standard shipping on orders over $150.
How should research compounds be stored?
Lyophilized peptide powders should be stored at -20°C for long-term storage or at 4°C for short-term use (up to 4 weeks). Protect from light and moisture. Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, store at 4°C and use within 4 weeks.
Do you offer bulk or institutional pricing?
Yes. We offer volume pricing for research institutions, universities, and licensed facilities. Contact our sales team with your institution credentials and research requirements for a custom pricing quote.
